Resources

Everything here is free. Some of it is ours, most of it belongs to the worm community, and all of it is what we would hand a new student on their first day.

Everything about our favorite lifeform

These are the references the lab works from daily.

  • WormBase. Genes, alleles, strains and literature. The first place to look.
  • WormAtlas. Anatomy, cell by cell, with electron micrographs.
  • WormBook. Peer-reviewed chapters covering the biology and the methods.
  • WormWiring. The reconstructed connectome, synapse by synapse.
  • Textpresso. Full-text search across the C. elegans literature.
  • Caenorhabditis Genetics Center. Where strains come from.
  • WormWeb connectome viewer. The nervous system as an interactive network.

What we have released

  • Behavioral turn analysis. Software for classifying turning strategies, released on Zenodo.
  • Animal heading calculator. Code for computing heading from tracked paths.
  • Affordable worm tracker. A tracking system costing about thirty dollars, published so classrooms can build one.
  • Burrowing assay. The method paper for assessing neuromuscular integrity and burrowing choice.
  • RGBCaMP and WINK, the reporter animal and the imaging and kinematics platform, are being prepared for release. Write if you want to use either before then.

For teachers and visitors

  • WormTV. Our video channel on C. elegans biology, watched more than 41,000 times.
  • Our book for children. Given away at outreach events and available to buy.
  • Outreach activities. The four sessions we run, with what each needs and how long it takes.
